Privacy Policy

MP3 Rabbit · Last updated 16 September 2026

This policy explains what MP3 Rabbit does with your information. MP3 Rabbit is an offline music player: it plays audio files that are already stored on your device. It is published by Fwoce Media.

The short version

MP3 Rabbit does not collect, upload or share your music, your listening history, or any personal information. Everything the app knows about your library stays on your device. The app shows advertising, and the advertising provider collects some information — that part is described below.

Information the app stores on your device

To work as a music player, MP3 Rabbit reads and stores the following on your device only. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

  • Details of audio files on your device: titles, artists, albums, genres, durations and cover art, read from the files themselves.
  • Playlists you create, and any corrections you make to song details.
  • Your settings, such as playback and equaliser preferences.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. Your music files are not modified or deleted by the app unless you explicitly ask it to.

Permissions

  • Access to audio files — to find and play the music on your device. This is the app's core function.
  • Internet access — used only to load advertising. The app does not send your music or library information over the internet.
  • Notifications — to show playback controls while a song is playing.

Advertising

MP3 Rabbit displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To show ads, and to measure and limit how often they appear, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Information about ads shown to you, including whether you interacted with them
  • crash logs and diagnostic information

This information is collected by Google, not by Fwoce Media. We do not receive your advertising identifier or any information that identifies you personally; we see only aggregate totals such as how many ads were shown.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices about advertising

  • Consent. Where required by law, the app asks for your consent before personalised advertising is shown. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Privacy settings inside the app.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID. Android lets you reset your advertising identifier, or delete it entirely, under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops apps receiving a stable identifier for advertising.

Children

MP3 Rabbit is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us at the address below.

Analytics

Fwoce Media does not run its own analytics in MP3 Rabbit. The Google AdMob SDK collects information about how you interact with ads, along with crash logs and diagnostic information. Google uses this for analytics, advertising, fraud prevention and app stability. It does not include your music, your library or your listening history.

Data sharing and retention

Fwoce Media does not collect personal information through this app, and so has none to share, sell or retain. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies, linked above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
